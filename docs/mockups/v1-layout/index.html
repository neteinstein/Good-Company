Good Company · new layout

Proposal for review (2026-11-14). Today the bar is Friends · Together · Coming up · Ideas · Settings, and three of those tabs are mostly lists of people, while circles, gift lists, “Your people” and the network map sit at the end of lists or in the ⋮ menu. This sheet proposes four tabs, Today · People · Circles · Plans, with Settings (still one scrolling page) reached from your photo in every tab’s header, four closeness levels without the word “circle”, a card that asks when you last saw someone instead of listing everyone as overdue, and circle chips on a person’s page. Colours, type and components are the app’s own; sample people are the demo set. These are proposals, not renders of the real app.

L1 · navigation

Four tabs that each do one job

Today is what needs you now (it absorbs Coming up and the catch-up parts of Friends and Together). People is the one list of everyone, sorted by closeness (the F5 proposal), with “Your people” on top. Circles brings the circles out of the bottom of Together. Plans holds plans, gift lists, “Who’s free for…?” and, with AI on, the weekly ideas, so there is no AI-only tab coming and going. Settings is your photo at the top right.

L2 · Today

Today, the last-seen card, and Settings with your photo

Today a person with no meetup logged counts as overdue from today, so Coming up lists everyone. People with nothing logged stop counting as overdue. Instead one card asks, one person at a time, when you last saw them. The answer is an approximate date that only starts their rhythm; it is not a meetup and doesn’t show on the timeline.

L3 · closeness

Four closeness levels, none called “circle”

Inner · Close · Friends · Acquaintances (PT: Íntimos · Próximos · Amigos · Conhecidos). “Circle” then means only the groups you make. Acquaintances is new: people you want to keep (birthdays, notes, gifts) without a catch-up rhythm, and they don’t count as real friends. Stored values stay as they are (INNER, CLOSE, GOOD), plus ACQUAINTANCE, so backups keep working.

L4 · friend page

Add and remove circles from a person’s page

Today the page shows the person’s circles as pills and editing needs a long press (a hidden gesture, against F2). The pills get an × and a “+ Circle” chip that opens every circle as checkboxes.
