Circles: a page for each circle

Proposal for review (2026-11-14). Today tapping a circle opens its editor, and the Circles tab also carries “Last time with…” and a list of memories that isn’t about circles. This sheet gives every circle its own page, like a person’s: who’s in it, what’s coming up (plans and reminders), and a timeline of the meetups it had (with who came) and what was shared with it. From the page you log a meetup, make a plan or add a reminder. The tab keeps only the circles. Colours, type and components are the app’s own; the people are the demo set. These are proposals, not renders of the real app.

What the Circles tab does now

The red numbers are the points raised.

P1 · the tab

Only the circles

The cards stay as they are (colour, emoji, people, last meetup or rhythm, next plan, “We met”, the ⋮ menu). Tapping a card opens the circle’s page instead of the editor. “Last time with…” and the memories go: meetups show on each circle’s page and on each person’s timeline.

P1 · the circle page

Who’s in it, what’s coming, what you did together

Built like a person’s page. The header is the circle’s cover photo, or its colour. Four actions: We met (the “We met” panel with the members listed, nobody ticked), Plan (the plan editor with the circle picked), Reminder (a reminder for the circle) and Edit. Then the members, what’s coming up, and the timeline, all on this one page.
