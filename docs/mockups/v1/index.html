Good Company · V1 mockups

Six pieces of the V1 scope, one band each. They use the app’s own colours, type and components, and follow the decisions dated 2026-11-13 in docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md. Sample people are the app’s demo circle. These are proposals for review, not renders of the real app.

Issue 1 · gap analysis #2–#11

Full friend profile

New “About” card under the quick actions: work, where they live, how you met and who introduced you, preferred channel and emails. Family grows beyond kids. Tastes are split into food, drink, doesn’t like and interests. Free custom fields at the end.

Issue 2 · gap analysis #15, #21, #22

Timeline and logging

One timeline per friend with every kind of entry: meetups (including group ones), calls, messages, emails, video calls, journal entries, What’s new and gifts. A single “Log” sheet records any of them with a dated note.

Issue 3 · decision D1 · gap analysis #40–#45

Circles

Saved groups become circles: free-form, emoji and colour, several per friend. Closeness (Inner, Close, Good friends) stays one value per friend. Circles filter Home and the A–Z list, log a group meetup in one tap and can remind you when the group hasn’t met.

Issue 4 · gap analysis #23 · decision D7

App-icon shortcuts

Long-press the app icon to log fast: “Log a meetup” plus the three people you were with most recently, with their photo or initials. Each opens the log sheet already filled in.

Issue 5 · decisions D6, C1

Share → Good Company saves attachments

Shared text is kept whole, a link as address and title (never fetched), a photo as a gallery reference plus a small thumbnail. On confirm the user says whether it counts as an interaction or is just an idea or note.

Issue 6 · decision D2

Import from the contact list

On first use, with the contacts permission, the app lists the phone’s contacts with checkboxes. Only ticked people become friends. Refusing the permission falls back to today’s system contact picker. Play policy for READ_CONTACTS must be checked first.
