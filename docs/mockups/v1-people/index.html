People tab: search first, one row of filters, no hearts

Proposal for review (2026-11-14), after the four-tab layout (#65–#72) shipped. Today the People tab stacks the circle chips, a “Log a meetup” card, the search field, “Your people” and the closeness chips before the first person, and every row has a heart that sets or clears the closeness. This sheet moves search under the header, drops the log card, keeps one row of filters (circles), drops the closeness chips (the list is already grouped by closeness, with counts) and gives everyone a closeness level. Colours, type and components are the app’s own; the people are the demo set. These are proposals, not renders of the real app.

What the People tab does now

Choir selected and the search open, so every piece is on screen at once. The red numbers are the six points raised.

P1 · layout

Search, “Your people”, circles, then everyone by closeness

One filter row: the circles. Closeness isn’t a second row of chips: it’s already how the list is grouped, so each heading shows its count and that is all. There is no closeness filter (and no folding); in A–Z each row still names its level. Picking a circle filters the list and keeps the grouping, so the 16-person choir reads as 2 close, 2 friends and 12 acquaintances.

P2 · data

Everyone has a level; the hearts go

The heart goes from the rows and from the person’s page. The closeness picker is the only control, and it no longer offers “Not set”: new people start as Acquaintances, and “real friend” means any level above that (the brief’s “define who are real friends” still holds). Acquaintances have no catch-up rhythm by default, just like people without a level today, so no reminder changes.
